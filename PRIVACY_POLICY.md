# Privacy Policy for FocusPulse Timer

**Effective Date:** October 26, 2026  
**Developer:** Carl Bernal (`carlbernal`)  
**Application:** FocusPulse Timer (`com.carlbernal.focuspulse`)

## 1. Overview
FocusPulse Timer is a standalone, offline Pomodoro and productivity timer utility. We respect your privacy and have designed FocusPulse Timer so that it **does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or non-personal user data**.

## 2. Information Collection and Use
- **Personal Data:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, locations, or any other personally identifiable information.
- **Usage & Analytics Data:** We do not use any analytics frameworks, crash reporters, or advertising SDKs.
- **Device Permissions:** FocusPulse Timer requires zero sensitive device permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage access).
- **Local Session Data:** Any task labels or timer session logs you enter are held temporarily in device memory on your phone and never leave your device.

## 3. Third-Party Services
FocusPulse Timer does not integrate with any third-party data processors, advertising networks, or external APIs.

## 4. Children’s Privacy
FocusPulse Timer does not knowingly or unknowingly collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
If we update this Privacy Policy in future versions of the application, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play Store listing page for FocusPulse Timer.
